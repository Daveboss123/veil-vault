# Veil Vault art brief

Use this brief with Gemini / Nano Banana or OpenAI Images. Generated source
images are production inputs, not runtime dependencies. Do not put API keys in
this repository or in the browser application.

## Visual language

- Editorial dark-fantasy screen print with restrained pixel-game geometry.
- Deep forest-black, bone ivory, acid chartreuse, oxidized copper, moon blue.
- Hard-edged shapes, subtle paper grain, dramatic negative space.
- No photorealism, glossy 3D, generic mobile-game rendering or neon cyberpunk.
- No text, letters, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI or cryptocurrency marks.
- Characters wear masks. Do not show real people or recognizable public figures.

## First generation: cast sheet

Generate this first, then use it as the character reference for every later
image:

> Character lineup for an original indie stealth-bluffing game called Veil
> Vault. Four full-body masked night runners standing separately, equal visual
> weight, front three-quarter view: The Unnamed wears an angular blank ivory
> hood mask with one thin vertical chartreuse mark; Fox wears a sharp rust-red
> fox mask and courier cloak; Moth wears a pale lunar-moth mask with wide graphic
> wing shapes and archivist robes; Rook wears a black architectural bird helmet
> and the damaged coat of a former city guard. Editorial linocut screen-print
> illustration, medieval fantasy mixed with subtle pixel geometry, hard-edged
> silhouettes, deep forest-black background, bone ivory, acid chartreuse,
> oxidized copper and desaturated moon blue, sparse grain, dramatic negative
> space, consistent proportions and lighting, original character design. No
> words, letters, logo, watermark, signature, weapons pointed at viewer,
> photorealism, glossy 3D, anime, cryptocurrency symbols, UI. 16:9.

## Individual portrait template

Run once for each runner, attaching the approved cast sheet as a reference:

> Waist-up dossier portrait of [CHARACTER NAME] from the attached Veil Vault
> cast sheet. Preserve the exact mask, costume, palette, proportions and graphic
> screen-print style. Character centered, looking slightly past camera, deep
> forest-black field with one geometric moon halo, hard-edged linocut shapes,
> sparse paper grain, generous empty margin, no text, no border, no logo, no
> watermark, no signature. 4:5 portrait.

Character notes:

- **The Unnamed:** guarded but curious; ivory hood mask; chartreuse key-line.
- **Fox:** confident courier; rust-red mask; asymmetric scarf; alert posture.
- **Moth:** quiet archivist; moon-blue and ivory wings; fine antenna silhouette.
- **Rook:** imposing ex-warden; black bird-like architectural helmet; worn coat.

## Environment key art

> Night panorama of Veyr, an original vertical river city controlled by a giant
> bell court. Three sealed vault districts glow faintly: an oxidized copper old
> mint, a moon-blue underground archive, and an ivory high court holding a small
> sun-colored crown. Four tiny masked runners cross separate rooftops toward the
> center. Editorial linocut screen print, medieval city planning diagram mixed
> with subtle pixel geometry, deep forest-black, bone ivory and acid chartreuse,
> sparse grain, dramatic negative space. No words, labels, logo, watermark,
> signature, photorealism, glossy 3D, cryptocurrency marks or UI. 16:9.

## Export and handoff

1. Download the original PNG files at the highest available resolution.
2. Put them in `art-source/inbox/`; do not rename or edit the originals first.
3. Do not upload wallet files, screenshots containing addresses, or repository
   contents to the image service.
4. The production pass will crop, remove metadata, convert to WebP, record the
   generator/model, and keep the bundled files local.
